\section{Retraction: the $K$ saturation is an indexing artifact}
\label{sec:claims}

The saturation reported in the endpoint tables of the superseded note
(\texttt{update\_document\_exact\_full\_U.tex}, Sec.~``Endpoint tables'') is not a
property of the exact symmetries. It is caused by three defects in the
document-exact pipeline, of which the first is decisive.

\subsection{The exact generators are not symmetries at most geometries}

\texttt{src/sto3g\_exact\_symmetries.py} stores the document generators as
hardcoded orbital \emph{indices}
($\mathrm{H_2O}$: $Q_{B1}=\{4\}$, $Q_{B2}=\{2,6\}$;
$\mathrm{N_2}$: $Q_{\pi x}=\{4,7\}$, $Q_{\pi y}=\{5,8\}$,
$Q_u=\{1,3,4,5,9\}$).
Those indices are correct only at the geometry for which the PDF was written.
Molecular-orbital energies cross along both scans, so at other bond lengths the
same indices name orbitals of different irreps and the resulting $Z$ products
do not commute with $H$.

The diagnostic is \texttt{reference\_weight\_sum}: the fraction of the FCI
reference captured by the retained sector eigenstates. It is $1$ exactly when
the pinned operators are genuine symmetries.

Three consequences.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item For $\mathrm{H_2O}$ the compact/saturated boundary coincides
        \emph{exactly} with the geometry at which the $3a_1/1b_1$ ordering
        swaps ($R=1.472$). Where the supports are valid, $\sum w_{\mathrm{ref}}
        = 1$, $K \in \{4,5,7\}$ and every point converges. Where they are not,
        $\sum w_{\mathrm{ref}}$ decays monotonically to $0.766$ and collapses
        to $0.024$ at $R = 2.5$, where the ordering is scrambled furthest
        ($Q_{B1}\!\to\!\{6\}$, $Q_{B2}\!\to\!\{3,5\}$).
  \item For $\mathrm{N_2}$ the hardcoded supports are already wrong at
        $R=1.2$: $3\sigma_g$ occupies index $4$, so the correct supports are
        $Q_{\pi x}=\{6,7\}$ and $Q_u=\{1,3,5,6,9\}$. Consequently
        $\sum w_{\mathrm{ref}} = 0$ at \emph{every} geometry: the retained
        space is orthogonal to the FCI reference. No $K$ can converge, and the
        search necessarily runs to the full retained dimension. The $K=3584$
        column carries no information about the selection methods.
  \item The apparently excellent $|\Delta E_{\mathrm{dec}}| = 6.4\times10^{-3}$
        at $\mathrm{H_2O}$ $R=2.5$ is spurious: with
        $\sum w_{\mathrm{ref}} = 0.024$ the decoupled energy is a minimum over
        a space that barely overlaps the ground state.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The sector filter leaves one generator unpinned}

\texttt{metrics.py} recomputes
$n_{\mathrm{exact}} = n_{\mathrm{spin}} +
(n_{\mathrm{spatial\,kept}} - n_{\mathrm{las}})$
using the \emph{pre-drop} LAS count. Because
$\operatorname{span}(\mathcal E\cup\mathrm{LAS})$ has full rank on the spatial
register, all-ones lies in that span; its interleaved qubit image equals
$P_\alpha \oplus P_\beta$, so exactly one combined row is dropped and the
arithmetic charges that drop to the exact block. Measured on the campaign
artifacts: $n_{\mathrm{exact}} = 3$ against document $r = 4$ ($\mathrm{H_2O}$)
and $4$ against $5$ ($\mathrm{N_2}$).
\texttt{filter\_labels\_fixed\_exact} therefore pins only the leading
$n_{\mathrm{exact}}$ generators, leaving $Q_{B2}$ and $Q_u$ free.

This explains the dimension mismatch flagged as an open question in the
previous draft. Independent determinant counting gives, for the fully pinned
document sector, $133$ ($\mathrm{H_2O}$) and $1824$ ($\mathrm{N_2}$), matching
the PDF. The observed retained dimensions are $261$ and $3584$: exactly the
dimensions obtained when one point-group generator is left unconstrained.
There is no $2^{r}$ tax; the working space was never correctly restricted.

\subsection{The sector label is chosen by size, not by physics}

Neither \texttt{trillium\_resubmit\_document\_exact\_U.sh} nor the endpoint
grid scripts export \texttt{EXACT\_SECTOR}, so
\texttt{choose\_default\_exact\_sector} selects the \emph{densest} physical
tapered sector. For $\mathrm{N_2}$ this lands on a sector carrying a $-1$
$\pi$ parity, which cannot contain the ${}^1\Sigma_g^+$ ground state --- the
mechanism behind $\sum w_{\mathrm{ref}} = 0$.

\subsection{Method discrimination was structurally impossible}

Independently of the above, $n_{\mathrm{sym}}$ is fixed at $5$
($\mathrm{H_2O}$) and $7$ ($\mathrm{N_2}$) while $r_{\mathrm{spatial}}$ is $2$
and $3$, so $r + n_{\mathrm{sym}} = N$ in both cases and
$\operatorname{span}(\mathcal E \cup \mathrm{LAS})$ is the whole GF(2) space.
The sector partition is then the finest possible and is identical for every
selection rule. The coincidence of all six method$\times$cost series in
the endpoint figures of the superseded note is a consequence of this
configuration, not evidence that the methods agree.

\subsection{Status of the campaign}

The $252$ endpoint metrics of this campaign do not support any conclusion
about iterative versus mixed pools, about the effect of exact symmetries on
$K$, or about full versus irrep orbital packing. The agreement between
full-$U$ and irrep-$U$ on $124/126$ paired points is expected once both arms
share a reference space that is largely (\,$\mathrm{N_2}$: entirely\,)
orthogonal to the target state.

Required before any rerun:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item derive the generator supports per geometry from the point-group labels
        of the Hartree--Fock orbitals in each \texttt{.chk}
        (\texttt{scripts/audit/gen\_exact\_from\_orbsym.py}), and assert
        $\sum w_{\mathrm{ref}} = 1$ as a run-time precondition;
  \item repair the $n_{\mathrm{exact}}$ arithmetic in \texttt{metrics.py} to
        use the post-drop LAS count, and assert
        $n_{\mathrm{exact}} = r_{\mathrm{spatial}} + 2$;
  \item pass \texttt{--exact\_sector} explicitly rather than relying on the
        density heuristic;
  \item choose $n_{\mathrm{sym}} < N - r$ so the partition is not maximal and
        the selection rules can be distinguished.
\end{enumerate}
