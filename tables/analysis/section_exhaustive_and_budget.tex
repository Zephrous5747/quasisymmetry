\section{The LAS budget violates Eq.~(6)}
\label{sec:budget-defect}

The campaign fixes $M = n_{\mathrm{sym}} = n_{\mathrm{singles}} +
n_{\mathrm{quartets}} = 5$ ($\mathrm{H_2O}$) and $7$ ($\mathrm{N_2}$). This is a
greedy quota, never checked against Eq.~(6), $M \le N - r$.

The exact parity matrix written to disk contains only the point-group rows. It
omits total particle parity,
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{1} \;=\; \prod_p \bar Z_p \;=\; \prod_p Z_{p\alpha} Z_{p\beta}
\;=\; (-1)^{\hat N} \;=\; P_\alpha \oplus P_\beta ,
\end{equation}
which is an exact symmetry and is already counted among the document's $r$
generators (as $P_\alpha$ and $P_\beta$). Because it never enters the GF(2)
independence test of Eq.~(7), the admissible budget is overstated by one:

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Admissible LAS budget in the spatial $\bar Z$ register.}
\label{tab:budget}
\begin{tabular}{lccccc}
\toprule
 & $n$ & rank $\mathcal E$ (PG only) & $M_{\max}$ & rank $\mathcal E$ (with $\mathbf 1$) & $M_{\max}$ \\
\midrule
$\mathrm{H_2O}$ & 7  & 2 & 5 & 3 & \textbf{4} \\
$\mathrm{N_2}$  & 10 & 3 & 7 & 4 & \textbf{6} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The runs therefore carry one LAS row that is independent only because
$\mathbf 1$ was dropped from $\mathcal E$. The same omission has two further
consequences already documented elsewhere in this note: it is the row that
\texttt{clifford\_symmetries\_from\_spatial} discards at the qubit level once
$P_\alpha, P_\beta$ are prepended (the source of the $n_{\mathrm{exact}}$
off-by-one), and it is why $r_{\mathrm{spatial}} + M = N$ exactly, which forces
$\operatorname{span}(\mathcal E \cup \mathcal G) = \mathbb F_2^{N}$ and makes
the sector partition maximal.

\subsection{Reachable candidate space}
Sec.~4.1 of the procedure note works in the qubit register, $N = 2n$:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccccc}
\toprule
 & $N$ & $r$ & $N-r$ & $|\mathcal C_{\mathrm{full}}| = 2^{N-r}-1$ & reachable here \\
\midrule
$\mathrm{H_2O}$ & 14 & 4 & 10 & 1023 & 4 \\
$\mathrm{N_2}$  & 20 & 5 & 15 & 32767 & 6 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The implementation represents LAS only as spatial products
$\bar Z_p = Z_{p\alpha} Z_{p\beta}$, which span $n$ of the $2n$ parity
directions. The reachable quotient is therefore $4$ / $6$ dimensional against
the document's $10$ / $15$: \emph{under half of the specified candidate space},
and the inaccessible directions are exactly the spin-resolved ones
($Z_{p\alpha}$ alone). This is a property of the candidate representation, not
a tuning choice, and it bounds every comparison in this note.

\section{Exhaustive quotient-pool control (Sec.~4)}
\label{sec:exhaustive}

All $2^{N-r}-1$ nontrivial parity classes modulo $\mathcal E$ were scored at
each point's converged orbitals ($31$ classes for $\mathrm{H_2O}$, $127$ for
$\mathrm{N_2}$ in the spatial register), the greedy independence scan was
repeated on the full ranking, and the result compared with the polynomial pool
(Sec.~4.3). Scoring uses
$([\widetilde H, Z(g)]\Psi)_i = (\widetilde H (z\!\cdot\!\Psi))_i - z_i
(\widetilde H\Psi)_i$, one Hamiltonian application per candidate.

A correctness gate re-scores the polynomial pool's own rows and compares
against its recorded \texttt{selected\_costs}; $121/126$ points reproduce them
to $\le 10^{-4}$ relative.

\subsection{The span test is vacuous at this budget}
$\operatorname{span}(\mathcal E \cup \mathcal G)$ agrees on $126/126$ points ---
but with $M = N-r$ \emph{any} $M$ independent rows span the whole quotient, so
the test cannot fail for any pool whatsoever. It is not evidence of anything.
For the same reason \texttt{first\_rejected\_competitor} is empty at every
point: the scan accepts every independent row it meets, so Sec.~4.3 item~2
(the NC gap to the first rejected independent competitor) has no content here
either. Both tests require $M < N-r$.

\subsection{What the control does establish}
The informative quantity is the total objective. Restricting to the $41$
iterative points where both pools are scored in the same frame,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\sum_i c(g_i^{\mathrm{exhaustive}})}
     {\sum_i c(g_i^{\mathrm{polynomial}})}
\in [1 - 5\times10^{-12},\, 1],
\qquad \text{identical to } 10^{-9} \text{ on } 41/41 .
\end{equation}
The polynomial current-frame library of $N(N+1)/2$ singles and pairs therefore
attains the \emph{same} minimum-NC quotient basis as scanning all $31$ / $127$
classes. Within the reachable candidate space and at $M = N-r$, the
restricted-neighbourhood approximation of Sec.~7.1 costs nothing for the
iterative pool.

The greedy/mixed pools cannot be compared from the same artifacts: their
recorded \texttt{selected\_costs} sum exactly to \texttt{cost\_before}, i.e.\
they are selection-time values at the \emph{unrotated} orbitals, because the
quota is chosen once before the orbital optimiser runs. For
$\mathrm{H_2O}$ at $R=1.1293$\,\AA, \texttt{cost\_before} $= 0.40683$ against
\texttt{cost\_after} $= 0.25362$: the mixed quota is selected against a
Hamiltonian $38\%$ different in objective from the one it is finally evaluated
in. This asymmetry with the iterative pool --- which reselects at every
macroiteration --- is a plausible mechanism for the $K$ differences in
Sec.~\ref{sec:iter-vs-mixed} that is independent of pool construction.

\subsection{Reference ambiguity at stretched $\mathrm{H_2O}$}
Five points fail the gate, all at $R \ge 2.33$\,\AA. At $R = 2.5$\,\AA\ the FCI
gap is $E_1 - E_0 = 4.5\times10^{-4}$\,Ha, so the reference is not determined by
energy alone and independent solvers can return different vectors from the
near-degenerate manifold. The NC and variance rankings at those geometries are
correspondingly reference-dependent; results there should be quoted with that
caveat rather than as converged values.

\subsection{Not attempted}
The Sec.~4.2 \emph{self-consistent} exhaustive fixed point --- re-optimising the
orbitals against the exhaustive pool and re-ranking until the LAS span stops
moving --- is implemented
(\texttt{scripts/exhaustive\_selfconsistent.py}) but not reported here, because
at $M = N-r$ it converges at the first macroiteration by construction. Sec.~4.4
is explicit that even the converged fixed point would not be a global optimum:
it removes candidate omission only, and local orbital minima, the basis
dependence of the generator-sum objective (Sec.~5), and cycles all remain.

\section{Reproducibility record (Sec.~10)}
\label{sec:repro}
All seven items are satisfied on $126/126$ points of the $U_{\mathrm{irrep}}$
grid; item~6 is not applicable (route A, no DMRG in the search).
Item~1's rotation convention is \emph{measured} rather than assumed: the
orbital rotation is applied as $C_{\mathrm{MO}} U^{\mathsf T}$, established by
requiring the recorded NC values to be reproduced --- $C_{\mathrm{MO}} U$ is
wrong by up to a factor of $78$, and is invisible to $E_{\mathrm{FCI}}$, which
is invariant under orbital rotation.

Remaining gaps are not record-keeping but unrun controls: the Sec.~4.2
self-consistent fixed point at $M < N-r$, the qubit-scope exhaustive pool
($1023$ / $32767$ classes), Sec.~7.3(4) boundary reranking with a more accurate
state, and the Sec.~3.2/7.1 multiple-initialisation robustness test.
